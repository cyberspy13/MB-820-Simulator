MB-820 SIMULATOR
================

Open the app: https://cyberspy13.github.io/MB-820-Simulator/


WHAT IS IT?
-----------

After passing the MB-820 exam (Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central
Developer), I decided to create MB-820 Simulator, a free practice exam app
that runs in your browser.

You import your own practice questions, take them as an exam, and get a score
report at the end. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

The app comes with 8 original sample questions to try. It does not include a
question bank.


WHAT CAN IT DO?
---------------

- Import your own questions from PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV and other
  files
- Practise single and multiple choice, matching, Yes/No, code completion and
  ordering questions
- Take a whole exam, chosen sections, a number of questions, a range, or only
  the questions you answered incorrectly before
- Use a timer, or switch it off
- Use training mode, which checks each answer as you go
- Skip questions, flag them for review, and show the answer
- Use the calculator and zoom
- Save an exam and resume it later
- Get a score report, then retake the incorrect, unanswered or flagged
  questions


HOW TO START
------------

To try the samples:
  1. Open the app and press "Load samples". This button is shown while your
     library is empty.
  2. Press "Start exam", choose your options and press "OK".
  3. Press "Begin".

To use your own questions:
  1. Press "Upload questions".
  2. Choose your files, or drop them into the window.
  3. Open "Check what was read" to make sure the questions look right.
  4. Press "Add to library".
  5. Press "Start exam", choose your options and press "OK".
  6. Press "Begin".

If the app does not find your questions, download the Word, Excel or text
template from the upload window and copy your questions into it.


WHICH FILES CAN I IMPORT?
-------------------------

- PDF
- Word (.docx, .doc)
- Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV
- PowerPoint (.pptx)
- OpenDocument (.odt, .ods, .odp)
- RTF, HTML, TXT and MD
- Files exported from this simulator (.json)

Not supported: scanned documents, .xlsb, .ppt, and pictures inside old .doc
and .xls files.


WHERE DO MY FILES GO?
---------------------

Your files are read in your browser and are not uploaded to a server. Your
questions, results and saved exams are kept in the browser you are using, so
they do not appear on other devices, and clearing your browser data removes
them. Use "Export" to keep a copy of your questions.
